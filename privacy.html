Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Block Jai handles information when you use the game.

Information You Provide

Block Jai does not require you to create a Block Jai account or provide your name, address, phone number, or other personal information directly to the developer in order to play the game.

Game Data

Block Jai may store game progress, settings, scores, virtual currency, unlocked features, and other gameplay information locally on your device.

Game Center

Block Jai uses Apple Game Center for features such as leaderboards. If you use Game Center, information such as your Game Center nickname, player identifier, scores, and related gameplay information may be processed by Apple according to Apple's privacy practices.

Advertising

Block Jai uses Google AdMob to provide advertising, including banner advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as your IP address, approximate location derived from your IP address, device identifiers, advertising information, app interactions, crash information, and performance information.

The information collected and used by Google may vary depending on your device settings, privacy choices, region, and whether you grant tracking permission.

App Tracking Transparency

Where applicable, Block Jai may ask for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before allowing information to be used for tracking across apps and websites owned by other companies.

You may decline this permission and continue using Block Jai.

In-App Purchases

Block Jai may offer optional in-app purchases. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer of Block Jai does not receive your full payment card information.

Third-Party Services

Block Jai uses services provided by third parties, including:

  • Apple App Store and StoreKit
  • Apple Game Center
  • Google AdMob

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Children's Privacy

Block Jai is not designed specifically to collect personal information from children. The developer does not knowingly request personal information directly from children through the game.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to Block Jai, third-party services, or applicable requirements. The latest version will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Block Jai's privacy practices, please visit the Block Jai Support page.
